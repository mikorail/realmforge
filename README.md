# Realmforge

Forge procedural worlds (continents, rivers, peoples, faiths, realms) stacked with underground holds, floating sky cities and undersea domes, across planets joined by rifts. Then watch history unfold, play god, or live as anyone in it. Eight genres from high fantasy to cyberpunk and solarpunk; 22 culture spheres from Asia, Africa, the Americas, Oceania and Europe.

## Features

- **Fantasy & Exotic Races (12 Species)**: Inhabit worlds populated by Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Orcs, Trolls, Beastmen, Halflings and Gnomes, Giants, Goblins, Dragonborn, Merfolk, and Starborn Celestials. Cultures and realms adopt authentic racial profiles with stat modifiers, lifespan multipliers (e.g. elves 3.5x, celestials 5.0x, dwarves 2.0x, giants 4.0x), preferred layers, biomes, and unique polity forms.
- **Constructible Wonders & Wonder Destruction**: Landmark architectural achievements and natural sanctuaries (The Great World Tree, High Spire of Sorcery, Tower of Trials, Titan's Bridge, Grand University, Giant Harbor, Golden Metropolis, Primeval Forge, Sunken Citadel, Titan Shrines). Wonders can be dynamically commissioned and built by peaceful AI realms, erected by rulers and avatars (Build Tower, Build Wonder deeds), or summoned via the Consecrate Wonder divine miracle. Wonders grant empire bonuses (innovation, stability, military power, trade) and render with glowing map badges on canvas. Wonders can also be sacked and destroyed during wartime city conquests, razed by conquerors and avatars, blasted by divine cataclysms, or demolished by anthropogenic disasters. Ruined wonders persist on the map with weathered ruin icons, recording their destroyer and ruin cause.
- **Tough Monsters & Primordial Titans**: Living beasts across 4 categories: Biblical Titans (Leviathan, Behemoth, Ziz), Local Guardian Legends (White Tiger, Golden Sun Lion, Emerald Qilin), Mythos Cryptids (Loch Monster, Thunderbird, Dread Wolf), and Dangerous Beasts (Ancient Red Dragon, Frost Drake, Abyssal Kraken, Titan Boar Herds). Features roaming behaviors, rampage events near settlements, dynamic health, and animated map badges. Formidable beasts possess damage-reduction armor (45-50% damage reduction on Cataclysm titans), annual health regeneration, and lethal counter-attacks, making them hard to kill.
- **Beast Slaying, Trophies & Battlefield Shrines**: Slaying legendary monsters by heroes, royal armies, or avatars yields fabled relics and weapons forged from their remains (dragonfang blades, leviathan carapace shields, titan bone hammers), material bounties in gold and innovation, realm stability surges (+20 stability), and consecrates permanent Titan Shrine wonders on the battlefield commemorating the victory.
- **Pantheon Patron Gods & Divine Proxy Wars**: Eight deities across celestial domains (Solarius of the Sun, Kaelok of War, Nerida of the Seas, Vaelith of Shadows, Ilyra of the Earth, Zephyros of Storms, Thalor of Wisdom, and Morvath of Death) actively back mortal realms as Patron Gods. Deities bestow domain miracles (war buffs, harvest windfalls, storm strikes, trade boons) and incite explosive Divine Proxy Wars when rival patron nations clash in mortal conflict. Players can crown their own Chosen Realm using the Crown Chosen Realm divine power.
- **Luminaries & Polymaths**: Spawns historical Archmages, Philosophers, Master Artisans, Grand Champions, and Master Artificers. Each luminary authors a Magnum Opus work, earns world renown, accelerates scientific innovation, and achieves historic milestones recorded in the annals.
- **Branching Technology Progression Tree**: Deep tech progression across 5 specialized disciplines (Warfare & Tactics, Arcana & Science, Engineering & Masonry, Agronomy & Medicine, Navigation & Transit). Unlocks 25 discoveries with explicit prerequisites and 75 landmark historic inventions across 5 epochs.
- **Political Ideologies & Dynamic Factions**: 7 distinct state ideologies (Hereditary Monarchy, Civic Republic, Holy Theocracy, Merchant Oligarchy, Iron Autocracy, Communal Democracy, Polytechnic Technocracy). Every realm hosts internal political factions and parties competing for influence, with militancy shifts driven by wars, economic crises, and stability fluctuations.
- **Dynamic Regime Shifts & Constitutional Transformations**: States can radically transform their governing systems into Republics, Democratic Unions, Free Unions, Confederations, Stratocracies, or Theocracies across multiple historical avenues:
  - *Ideological Revolts & Rebellions*: Dissident provinces break away under distinct revolutionary doctrines (republican burghers, agrarian communal unions, mercantile leagues, theocratic zealots), carving out newly titled ideological states (such as Republics, Communes, and United Provinces).
  - *Popular Mass Revolutions*: Soaring faction militancy and low stability trigger nationwide general uprisings that topple autocratic monarchies, dismantle old thrones, and establish Democratic Unions or People's Republics.
  - *Peaceful Constitutional Reforms*: Enlightened monarchs and national assemblies peacefully ratify constitutional charters without bloodshed, transitioning stable nations into Federal Republics or Democratic Unions.
  - *Post-War Enforced Treaties*: Decisive military victories empower conquerors to impose sister ideological constitutions upon defeated rivals at swordpoint.
  - *Dynastic Interregnum Throne Abolition*: When rulers die leaving no living heirs, disillusioned citizens and republican assemblies can vote to permanently abolish the crown rather than accept foreign pretenders, establishing a Republic or Commonwealth.
  - *Avatar Deeds & Proclamations*: Playing as an avatar, rulers can issue sovereign decrees to "Proclaim Republic", "Form Free Union", or "Crown Sovereign", while non-ruling citizens inside the capital can "Ignite Revolution" to overthrow the regime.
- **Palace Coups d'État**: Discontent and high faction militancy trigger palace coups, overthrowing rulers, transforming constitutions, and re-titling realm forms.
- **Randomized Societal Uprisings, Cults, Prophets & Folk Heroes**: Living societies experience spontaneous popular uprisings and turbulent grassroots movements driven by war weariness, economic ruin, high taxation, and low stability:
  - *Urban & Agrarian Riots*: Bread riots sparked by famine, violent tax revolts against greedy crowns, draft resistance against grinding wars, and sectarian riots in religiously divided cities.
  - *Secret Esoteric Cults*: Doomsday millenarian sects awaiting celestial cataclysms, eldritch void worshippers in shadowy alleys, outlaw blood covens, and fanatical flagellant orders wandering plague-ridden provinces.
  - *Charismatic Prophets & Religious Awakenings*: Ascetic reformers, militant holy crusaders, pacifist mystics, and miracle workers emerge among commoners, drawing massive pilgrim followings, elevating national piety, and challenging decadent state clergy.
  - *Folk Heroes & Outlaw Champions*: Greenwood outlaws defying tyranny, arena gladiator rebels, fearless monster slayers, and noble border paladins rise to rally commoners and defend the downtrodden.
  - *Garrison Mutinies*: Unpaid regiments and disillusioned mercenaries revolt in wartime, seizing border fortresses and pillaging regional treasuries.
  - *Avatar Subversion & Leadership*: As an avatar, players can actively take up the cause of unrest: Incite Riots to destabilize rival regimes, Found Secret Cults to amass occult influence, Proclaim Prophecies to sway the masses, or Stand as a Folk Hero to inspire righteous rebellion.
- **Civilization-Induced Ecological Disasters**: Mortal activity alters the land: reckless timber clearcutting ignites rampant wildfires transforming forests to charred plains; canal dykes burst in monsoon rains to flood river valleys; deep-vein mining fissures trigger cave-ins; over-farming dust bowls exhaust arid soils; runaway arcane leyline meltdowns reduce advanced cities to ash; and river reservoir dam ruptures drown lowland bridges and settlements.
- **Faith Destruction, State Atheism & The Great Reformation**: Mortal idealism can topple sacred institutions. Radical ideological revolutions (Communal Democracy, Civic Republic, Polytechnic Technocracy) can decree State Atheism, stripping state religions, repudiating patron gods, secularizing society, and seizing church vaults (+65 gold to national treasury). Furthermore, theological schisms ignite the Great Reformation, spreading reformed doctrines and inciting iconoclast uprisings that dismantle traditional shrines across conservative empires.
- **Clandestine Intrigue & Espionage**: Rival nations conduct 5 covert operations: Sabotage Armories, Incite Sedition, Court Assassinations, Siphon Treasury, and Sow Diplomatic Paranoia. Botched missions trigger explosive diplomatic scandals and broken pacts.
- **Theologies & Religious Doctrines**: Religions develop unique theologies (Monotheism, Polytheism, Animism, Pantheism, Dualism) and religious doctrines (Caste Hierarchy, Evangelical Zeal, Holy Crusade, Universal Compassion, Sacred Freedom, Esoteric Mysteries, Ancestor Veneration), defining spiritual traditions and clergy titles.
- **Story Quests & Multi-Stage Expeditions**: Living heroes embark on narrative quests across 4 archetypes (Monster Subjugation, Relic Recovery, Hostage Rescue, Ruin Expedition). Features multi-stage story progression (Scouting, Confrontation, Triumph), chronicle entries, and rewards including relics and renown.
- **Traders, Entrepreneurs & Market Ventures**: Inhabit the world as merchants, traders, or entrepreneurs. Avatars can invest venture capital in caravan routes for gold returns, charter commercial guilds and merchant banks (+treasury, +innovation), or launch covert infiltration missions into foreign citadels.
- **Automated Random Divine Casting**: Cast any divine power instantly on an automatically resolved appropriate target without manual map clicking, plus trigger surprise divine miracles for unexpected celestial interventions.
- **Cartographer & DM Studio**: Export high-resolution print posters (PNG) with decorative cartouche, neatline frame, compass rose, and scale bar. Download layered vector maps (SVG) for tabletop VTTs and vector editors. Generate comprehensive Markdown gazetteer dossiers of all realms, rulers, pantheons, and annals.
- **Shareable Seed Links**: Encode seeds, planetary classifications, and climate setups into compact, shareable URL hash links that load directly in the generator.
- **Living History & Dynasties**: Trace noble lineages and ancestral roots with the interactive royal family tree explorer. Inspect royal house rosters, living heirs, traits, and causes of death.
- **Sacred Relics & Artifacts**: Forge or discover legendary weapons, crowns, tomes, and amulets that grant stat bonuses and fame. Plunder sacred heirlooms when enemy capitals fall.
- **Living Economy & Trade Caravans**: Regional commodity production nodes (grain, timber, iron, silver, spices, silk, crystals, petroleum) based on climate and altitude. Animated trade caravans travel along roads and sea lanes.
- **Marching Armies & Fronts**: Contested borders feature marching army regiment tokens with visible movement vectors during active wars.
- **Chronicle Timeline Scrubber**: Periodic history snapshots every 5 years allow scrubbing back and forth through past eras to watch borders expand and collapse without altering ongoing simulations.
- **Complete Chronicle Simulation & Full History Archive**: Fast-forward simulation mode with selectable epochs (25, 50, 100, 200, or 500 years) running smoothly in the background with a live progress bar and non-blocking frame pacing. Retains complete, unbounded historical annals (allEvents) and up to 1000 timeline snapshots from Year 1 to the end without truncation. Automatically commits finished simulations to IndexedDB, supports instant timeline scrubbing across the entire span of civilizational history, and offers a one-click "Download Complete Annals Book (MD)" for full illuminated chronicles.
- **Grand Chronicle Annals & Literary Prose Narration**: Procedural history chronicles are woven into rich, varied historical literature reminiscent of grand illuminated annals (Gibbon, Anglo-Saxon Chronicle, Silmarillion annals). Experience procedural war pretexts, citadel sieges, relic lootings, dynastic extinctions, concordat treaties, child regencies, midnight palace coups, and celestial omens. Switch seamlessly between an illuminated annual storybook ("Annals") and an interactive event log ("Timeline"), copy lore with one click, or export narrative annals directly in Markdown gazetteer dossiers.
- **Cross-Planet Rift Wars & Multiverse Invasions**: Tear cosmic gateways between worlds with the Tear Rift divine power. Expansionist realms stage interplanetary vanguard invasions, storm foreign capitals, and establish Rift-Dominions beyond the stars.
- **Subterranean Ghost Peeking**: Toggle contour ghost overlays in the layer bar to peek at surface coastlines from deep caverns, or inspect cavern bounds from the surface.
- **Expanded God Powers (25 Divine Powers)**: Unleash Chaos, Great Pestilence, Apocalypse Omen, Awaken Beast, Spawn Villain, Anoint Prophet, Spawn Species, Alien Invasion, Tear Rift, Enlighten, Forge Relic, Crown Chosen Realm, and Tectonic Cataclysm across 4 divine spheres. Inhabit the world in Avatar mode as an Anointed Prophet preaching the gospel or a feared Dread Overlord commanding dark warbands.
- **Customise Universe**: Map size, 1K to 200K points, heightmap templates, planet types, sea levels, rivers, culture spheres, and exotic layers (underground holds, sky isles, undersea domes).
- **Configure World**: Real-time climate modeling for equator/pole temperatures, precipitation, wind bands, and globe mapping.
