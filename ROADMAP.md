# Realmforge roadmap

## Done (2026-10-14)
- Azgaar-style pipeline: Voronoi grid, template heightmaps, climate, rivers, biomes, cultures, burgs, states, faiths, roads/sea lanes.
- 22 culture spheres: East/South/SE Asian, African (Sahel, Guinea, Bantu-Swahili, Ethiopic), American (Nahua, Maya, Andean, Turtle Island, Boreal), Oceanic, European, Eldritch.
- 8 genres re-skinning forms, settlement kinds, layers, vocabulary, map themes.
- Exotic layers: N underground levels (holds/undercities and tunnels), sky isles (floating cities and sky-lanes), undersea domes; per-layer realms, shafts/docks, and cross-layer raids.
- Multiverse: up to 5 planets of 8 kinds, rifts, rift colonies, and avatar rift travel.
- Simulation engine: economy, stability, diplomacy, alliances, wars with live fronts, sieges of capitals, rebellions, coups, successions, plague, faith spread, heresies, tribes.
- God mode (25 powers including awaken beast, consecrate wonder, relic, cataclysm, chaos, apocalypse; mana/sandbox modes).
- Avatar mode: any person, travel network, encounters, trade, court, revolts, coups, ruling, prophet preaching, villain extortion, beast hunting, and tower/wonder construction.
- Fantasy & Exotic Races: 12 distinct species (Human, Elf, Dwarf, Orc, Troll, Beastman, Halfling/Gnome, Giant, Goblin, Dragonborn, Merfolk, Starborn Celestial) with stat modifiers, lifespan multipliers (elves 3.5x, dwarves 2.0x, celestials 5.0x, giants 4.0x), biomes, preferred layers, and polity forms.
- Wonders of the World & Constructible Towers: Landmark archetypes (Great World Tree, Tower of Magic, Tower of Trials, Colossus Bridge, Grand University, Giant Harbor, Golden Metropolis, Primeval Forge, Sunken Citadel, Titan Shrines) that can be generated at origin, commissioned by peaceful AI realms, built by rulers and avatars, or consecrated via divine miracles.
- Tough Monsters, Primordial Titans & Slaying Trophies: 13 creatures across 4 categories (Biblical Titans, Local Guardians, Mythos Cryptids, Dangerous Beasts) with damage-reduction armor (45-50% on Cataclysm titans), annual health regeneration, and counter-attacks. Slaying beasts rewards heroes and realms with legendary forged relics and weapons, material bounties, +20 realm stability, and permanent Titan Shrine wonders.
- Celebrated Luminaries & Polymaths: Historical Archmages, Philosophers, Master Artisans, Grand Champions, and Master Artificers with Magnum Opus masterpieces, traits, scientific innovation generation, and achievements.
- Branching Tech Tree & Inventions: 5 disciplines (Warfare, Arcana, Engineering, Agronomy, Navigation), 25 discoveries with explicit prerequisites, and 75 landmark historic inventions.
- Political Ideologies & Factions: 7 state ideologies (Monarchy, Republic, Theocracy, Oligarchy, Autocracy, Communal, Technocracy) with internal rival factions, parties, and influence/militancy dynamics.
- Dynamic Constitutional Transformations & Republic/Union Revolts: Kingdoms and empires can transform into Republics, Democratic Unions, Free Unions, Confederations, Stratocracies, or Commonwealths through ideological provincial revolts, popular mass revolutions, peaceful constitutional reforms, victor-imposed post-war treaties, interregnum crown abolition upon heirless monarch deaths, and player avatar proclamations/uprisings with dynamic regime titles, chronicle annals, and UI badges.
- Complete Chronicle Simulation & Full History Archive: Interactive fast-forward simulation mode running up to 500 years in non-blocking batches with progress display, preserving all annual annals and timeline snapshots from Year 1 to the end without truncation, with automatic IndexedDB saving, complete annals Markdown book export, and full historical border scrubbing.
- Randomized Societal Uprisings, Cults, Prophets & Folk Heroes: Spontaneous social upheavals including bread riots, tax revolts, conscription strikes, sectarian riots, secret doomsday and eldritch cults, charismatic prophets inspiring pilgrimages and peace movements, folk heroes and outlaw champions defending the common folk, garrison mutinies, and avatar uprising deeds.
- Regime Shifts & Palace Coups d'État: Coups triggered by high faction militancy and low stability, overthrowing rulers, transforming constitutions, and re-titling realm forms.
- Civilization-Induced Ecological Disasters: Anthropogenic catastrophes (reckless clearcutting wildfires, canal/dyke failure floods, deep mine fissure cave-ins, and over-farming dust bowls) with biome conversions and demographic fallout.
- Clandestine Intrigue & Espionage: 5 covert operations (Sabotage Armories, Incite Sedition, Court Assassinations, Siphon Treasury, Sow Diplomatic Paranoia) with spymaster checks, detection risks, and diplomatic scandals.
- Unique Theologies & Religious Doctrines: 5 theologies (Monotheism, Polytheism, Animism, Pantheism, Dualism) and 7 doctrines (Caste Hierarchy, Evangelical Zeal, Holy Crusade, Universal Compassion, Sacred Freedom, Esoteric Mysteries, Ancestor Veneration) with clergy titles and spiritual tenets.
- Story Quests & Multi-Stage Expeditions: Narrative heroic adventures across 4 archetypes (Monster Subjugation, Relic Recovery, Hostage Rescue, Ruin Expedition) with multi-stage progression (Scouting, Confrontation, Triumph), real-time logs, and tangible rewards.
- Traders & Entrepreneurs: Avatar mercantile careers with caravan investments, chartered commercial trading companies and banks (+treasury, +innovation, +stability), and infiltration actions into foreign citadels.
- Automated Random Divine Targeting: Cast any divine power on an automatically resolved appropriate target without manual map clicking, plus trigger Surprise Divine Miracles.
- Cartographer & DM Studio: Print-ready Ultra-HD PNG poster export (decorative cartouche, neatline, compass rose, metric scale bar), layered vector SVG export, Markdown gazetteer dossiers.
- Shareable seed links: Compact URL hash encoding/decoding for instant generator sharing.
- Living Dynasties: Interactive lineage family tree viewer, royal house rosters, and ancestral tracking.
- Sacred Relics & Heirlooms: Procedural artifacts with stat bonuses, fame, origin lore, and looting on capital capture.
- Living Economy & Marching Armies: Commodity nodes (grain, timber, iron, silver, spices, silk, crystals, petroleum), animated trade caravans along roads/sea routes, and regiment tokens along war fronts.
- Chronicle Timeline Scrubber: Automated 5-year history snapshots and non-destructive historical border playback slider.
- Cross-Planet Rift Wars: Interplanetary vanguard invasions, dynamic rift creation, capital seizures, and Rift-Dominion colonies.
- Subterranean Ghost Peeking: Non-destructive dashed contour overlays connecting surface coastlines and deep cavern bounds across layers.
- Chaos, Apocalypse, and Cosmic Pantheons: 25 divine powers across 4 spheres (Grace & Destiny, Earth & Genesis, Chaos & Calamity, Cosmos & Apocalypse) including Unleash Chaos, Great Pestilence, Apocalypse Omen, Awaken Beast, Spawn Villain, Anoint Prophet, Spawn Species, Alien Invasion, and Crown Chosen Realm.
- Wonder Destruction & Ruin Sites: Wonders can be sacked during city conquests, razed by conquerors/avatars, blasted by cataclysms, or destroyed by anthropogenic disasters (arcane meltdowns, reservoir dam ruptures). Sacked wonders remain on the map as weathered ruins with attribution.
- Pantheon Patron Gods & Divine Proxy Wars: 8 deities across celestial domains (Solarius, Kaelok, Nerida, Vaelith, Ilyra, Zephyros, Thalor, Morvath) sponsoring mortal realms, bestowing domain miracles, and igniting Divine Proxy Wars between opposing patron realms.
- Faith Destruction, State Atheism & The Great Reformation: Revolutionary regimes decree State Atheism, nationalizing church treasuries and repudiating deities. Theological schisms trigger the Great Reformation, spreading reformed doctrines and iconoclast uprisings.
- Grand Chronicle Annals & Literary Prose Narration: Atmospheric procedural history chronicle engine (Gibbon and medieval annals styling) with rich pretexts for wars, capital citadel sieges, relic plunder, dynastic extinctions, concordat treaties, child regencies, and midnight palace coups. Toggleable illuminated storybook ("Annals") and interactive timeline ("Timeline") in InfoPanel, one-click lore copying, and narrative Markdown gazetteer exports.
- IndexedDB saves, vitest test suites, headless gen/sim verification scripts.
- Big-map panning via snapshot caching.
